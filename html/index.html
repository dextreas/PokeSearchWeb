Squirtle (Japanese: ゼニガメZenigame) is a Water-type Pokémon introduced in Generation I as one of the 3 three starter pokemon you recieve.

It has 2 evolutoinary stages, Wartortle at level 16 and Blastoise at level 36.

Squirtle is available as a starter pokemon at the beginning of Pokémon Red, Green, Blue, FireRed, and LeafGreen.
